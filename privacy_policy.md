# Privacy Policy for Pandoo

*Last updated: January 8, 2025*

Team Super Panda ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Pandoo mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

## Data Collection and Use

Pandoo is designed with privacy in mind. We want to be clear about how the App handles data:

- **Local Storage Only**: All data (lists and items) is stored locally on your device using Hive database. No data is transmitted to external servers.
- **No Personal Information**: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.
- **No Analytics**: We do not implement any analytics or tracking tools.
- **No Advertisements**: The App does not display advertisements or use ad-related tracking.

## App Permissions

The App requires minimal permissions to function:
- **Storage**: Required only to save your lists and items locally on your device.

## Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device:
- Your lists and items remain private and accessible only through your device
- Data security is managed by your device's built-in security features
- Uninstalling the App will remove all associated data from your device

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

Since we don't collect any personal data, there is no personal information for us to manage. Your lists and items remain entirely under your control on your device.
