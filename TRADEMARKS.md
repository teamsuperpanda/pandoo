# Trademark and Brand Policy

The Pandoo source code is [MIT licensed](LICENSE). The artwork is
[CC BY 4.0](ASSETS-LICENSE.md). Neither licence grants trademark rights.

This policy explains what a fork may reuse and what it must replace, so that
users can always tell an official build from a modified one.

## What this policy covers

- The name "Pandoo" and the Pandoo wordmark.
- The panda mark, app icon, adaptive icon layers and launcher artwork.
- Splash screen art, the GitHub banner and store screenshots.
- Store listing copy, the Play Store and App Store records, bundle identifiers
  (`com.teamsuperpanda.pandoo`) and signing keys.

These belong to Team Super Panda.

## What you may do

- Use the code under the MIT licence, including commercially.
- Use the artwork under CC BY 4.0, including commercially, with attribution.
- Say that your project is "based on Pandoo" or "a fork of Pandoo", provided
  the reference is descriptive and does not imply endorsement.

## What a fork must do

If you distribute a modified build:

1. Choose a different app name and a different store listing.
2. Replace the app icon, splash art and any Pandoo branding in your build.
3. Use your own bundle identifier and your own signing key.
4. Keep the MIT copyright notice and the CC BY 4.0 attribution for any artwork
   you do reuse outside the app.
5. Do not use the Pandoo name or panda mark as the name or icon of your app.

## What you may not do

- Ship a build named "Pandoo" or using the panda mark without written
  permission.
- Publish to a store under Team Super Panda's developer accounts or bundle
  identifiers.
- Suggest that Team Super Panda endorses, maintains or supports your fork.

## Asking for permission

Open an issue at
https://github.com/teamsuperpanda/pandoo/issues or contact Team Super Panda
through https://www.teamsuperpanda.com. Permission is granted case by case and
in writing.
